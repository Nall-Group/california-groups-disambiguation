The California Association of Health Plans (CAHP), the Association of California Life and Health Insurance Companies (ACLHIC), and America's Health Insurance Plans (AHIP), write that under the ACA, states are required to select an essential health benefit (EHB) plan that defines the health-related services and equipment that health plans and insurance policies must cover. The benchmark plan selected by the Legislature in California did not include hearing aids. CHBRP has confirmed that coverage for hearing aids and associated services mandated by this proposal would exceed EHBs, and therefore trigger the ACA requirement that the state defray the cost of additional benefit coverage for enrollees in QHPs in Covered California. CHBRP estimates that this bill could translate to a state-responsibility of $11,268,000 annually. It's also important to note that this would be the first time since the passage of the ACA that California could pass a coverage mandate that exceeds the EHBs. The opposition is concerned about the precedent that this action may set, which could trigger a slew of new mandates to be introduced that exceed the EHBs, requiring more state funding to defray these costs. Additionally, this mandate is projected to cost enrollees approximately $52 million in increased premiums. CAHP, ACLHIC, and AHIP are also concerned about this bill's potential to harm affordability for small businesses and individual market consumers
