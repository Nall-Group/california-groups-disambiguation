# SUPERVISOR DECISIONS (apply to ALL tasks 1209-1223):
#  * TWO separate institution canonicals: "State Bar of California" AND "California Lawyers Association"
#    are distinct top-level canonicals (legally separate entities).
#  * In 2018 the State Bar's voluntary practice SECTIONS spun off into the independent CLA. So
#    "...Section of the State Bar of California" and "...Section of the California Lawyers Association"
#    are usually the SAME body. Merge both spellings into ONE section node.
#  * PRACTICE SECTIONS (Business Law, Family Law, Trusts & Estates, Taxation, Litigation, Environmental,
#    Public Law, Real Property, Criminal Law, IP, Legal Services, Privacy, Workers' Comp) => nest as a
#    `chapter` under the `California Lawyers Association` canonical; keep "...State Bar of California"
#    spellings as alternate_spelling within the section node. Committees => sub-chapters under their Section.
#  * REGULATORY committees that did NOT move to CLA (Committee on Administration of Justice, Committee on
#    Appellate Courts, ADR Committee, Standing Committee on Professional Responsibility, Justice & Diversity
#    Center, Conference of Delegates, Board of Trustees) => stay as chapters under `State Bar of California`.
#  * Each task is SELF-CONTAINED (tasks have no ordering): if the parent canonical (State Bar of California
#    or California Lawyers Association) does not yet exist, CREATE it, then nest. Keep the cleanest/most
#    complete spelling as each node's canonical name; everything else becomes alternate_spelling.
#  * These are consolidations WITHIN the crosswalk (no CSV changes). Run the cleaning/dedup/stats pipeline
#    before committing.
#  * Line numbers (Lxxxxx) are approximate locations at audit time — re-grep to confirm.
